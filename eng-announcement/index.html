Announcement (ver.261023)

These articles may be updated with new or improved content.
Please check the version to make sure you’re learning from the latest information.
Content in languages other than Korean is translated using AI.

One thing I found inconvenient about learning from blogs was that
it wasn’t easy to jump straight to the specific concept or piece of information I needed, check it quickly, and move on.

I often had to read an entire article just to find what I was looking for. To make that easier on this blog,
once I’ve built up a collection of tutorial posts, I’ll try publishing separate articles for individual key concepts. In other words, I’ll also post short articles that each focus on just one specific piece of knowledge.

So, with that said… I’d really appreciate a little support through the button below to help me keep this blog going for a long time… hehe.
